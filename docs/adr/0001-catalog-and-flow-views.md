# Catalog and Flow are separate views

The skill viewer shows two modes instead of one mixed graph: Catalog (files and references) and Flow (runtime steps). A single mixed view made the catalog look like a flow; that was the core complaint.

## Status

accepted

## Consequences

The old “Skill graph” and “Ticket pipeline” tabs are renamed or merged under these two terms. Parsers produce two artefacts, not one star-shaped graph.
